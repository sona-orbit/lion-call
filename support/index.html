Support

If something in Lion on the Line does not work as expected, check the steps below. If the answer is not here, write to us.

Emailsona.orbit.hi@gmail.com

How to use it

  1. Open the app. The first time, three short screens explain how it works. On the last one, pick the language the characters speak and download its voice pack (you can skip this for now), then tap Start.
  2. On the home screen, choose a character, one of its tasks, the number of times, and when the call should come.
  3. Under Ring On, choose This iPhone or the paired second device.
  4. Tap Call. With one phone, put it in front of your child.
  5. Each time your child does the task, mark it: with one phone, press and hold the next empty spot at the bottom of the call screen for about half a second; with two devices, tap Did it.
  6. To end early: with one phone, press and hold the faint little icon in the bottom-left corner for 1.5 seconds; with two devices, tap End Gently.

Linking a second device

  1. Open the app on both devices, go to Link Another Device in Settings and turn it on. The first time, iOS asks for Local Network access. Choose Allow.
  2. Give each device a name, such as “living room iPad”.
  3. On one device, tap the other in the list. A 4-digit code appears on the other device; type it on the first one.
  4. Once paired, they connect by themselves. Each device remembers one partner; you can unpair on the same page.
  5. To make your child’s device a phone booth, tap Use as Phone Booth on its home screen. To leave the booth, press and hold the faint little icon in the bottom-left corner for 2 seconds.

The two devices do not connect

  • Both must be on the same Wi-Fi network. If one is on mobile data or a guest network, they cannot find each other.
  • The app must be open and on screen on both devices. When it goes to the background it disconnects, and it reconnects when you return.
  • Check Local Network access: in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, make sure Lion on the Line is on. If access was denied, the app shows an explanation and an Open Settings button.
  • Some routers have device isolation turned on (also called AP isolation or client isolation), so devices on the same Wi-Fi cannot see each other. Turn it off in the router’s settings, or use a network without it.
  • If it still fails, unpair on both devices and pair them again.

No sound

  • Turn the volume up. The app plays sound in silent mode, but not with the volume all the way down.
  • With two devices, the app must be open and on screen on your child’s device, for example on the phone booth. It does not ring in the background or when the screen is locked.
  • If Bluetooth headphones or a speaker are still connected, the sound goes there.
  • If the characters sound like the phone’s standard system voice, the voice pack for that language has not been downloaded. Download it under Character Language in Settings.

Reporting a problem

In the app’s Settings, tap Report a Problem, describe what happened and tap Send. Leave an email address if you would like a reply. Up to 5 reports can be sent per hour.
