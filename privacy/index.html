Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This page explains how Lion on the Line (the iPhone and iPad app) and this website handle data.

Account

Lion on the Line needs no account and does not ask for your name or email address.

Your child

The app does not collect your child’s name, voice or photos. It does not use the microphone or the camera.

Call records

Call records (date, character, task, times done, how the call ended), the completion count for each character and your settings are stored only on the device and are not sent anywhere. You can delete all records in the app’s Settings.

Between two devices

Once you link a second device, the two send commands and status (such as ring, done once, end, call Mom or Dad) directly to each other within the same local network. Nothing passes over the internet or through our servers. This uses the iOS Local Network permission, which is only requested when you turn on Link Another Device; if you use one phone, you are never asked. Pairing details (the other device’s name and the pairing key) are kept in the device’s keychain.

When the app goes online

In two cases only. First, voice packs: the app reads the list of voice packs on this website and downloads the language you choose. These are plain web requests that carry no data about you or your child; the pack is stored on the device, is not backed up to iCloud, and can be deleted in Settings. Second, problem reports: only when you tap Send, the app sends your description, the email address you choose to give, the app version, the system version, the device model, the interface language, and a random number created at install time that is used only to limit reports to 5 per hour. These are written to the developer’s Google spreadsheet and used to find the problem and reply to you.

Ads and analytics

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website, not to the app. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Terms of use

Use of Lion on the Line is covered by Apple’s standard end user license agreement (EULA): www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Contact
